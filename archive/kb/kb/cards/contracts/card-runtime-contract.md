# Card runtime contract

Cards are equipable run modifiers purchased with Gems. Baseline constraints:
- cards cost Gems
- cards have a max level of 7
- card slots cap equipped count
- cards with lock status cannot be swapped mid-run while locked
- when a boss or fleet is alive, cards are locked until the boss/fleet is cleared

Use tables for card identities, slot costs, and mastery ladders. Use this note for semantics.
