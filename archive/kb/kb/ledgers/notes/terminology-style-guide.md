# Terminology style guide

## Purpose
Keep active KB language clear for browsing and GPT retrieval while preserving source fidelity in raw artifacts.

## Rules
- Prefer the canonical term in active filenames, headings, indexes, contracts, and overview notes.
- Short forms are allowed in compact prose, formulas, table cells, and source-transcribed structures only if they appear in `terminology-aliases.md`.
- Preserve original wording in `sources/raw/` and direct quotations.
- If a wiki page title is the clearest public term, use that as the canonical active term unless the KB has an explicit override. The active override for this KB is **Enhancements** over older Workshop Plus wording.
- Do not introduce new abbreviations without adding them to the alias ledger.

## Active naming preference
Use full names where practical:
- Enhancements
- Ultimate Weapons

- Black Hole
- Golden Tower
- Death Wave
- Chrono Field
- Inner Land Mines

- Poison Swamp
- Smart Missiles

- Effective Paths

## Compact-use allowance
Allowed compact forms include:
- UW, UW+
- BH, GT, DW, CF, ILM, CL, PS, SM, SL
- GB, FB
- EALS, EHLS, ELS
- EP

These are acceptable only when the surrounding context is already clear or when a table or contract would become awkward with full forms repeated everywhere.
