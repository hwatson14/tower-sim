# Wiki Enemy and Wave Baselines

Status: rebuilt from prior chat notes. This file captures wiki-backed baselines that were previously added in KB v6.
Confidence: mixed. Facts stated as explicit rules or values below were previously treated as wiki-backed. Any broader interpretation is marked as guidance.

## Enemy categories and caps
- Four enemy categories were recorded: Normal, Elite, Fleet, Boss.
- Simultaneous on-screen cap recorded: 150 total.
- Split recorded: 120 Normal, 20 Elite, 10 Boss.
- Fleet enemies were noted as consuming the Normal cap.

## Shared enemy mechanics
- Heat-up rule recorded: enemy damage increases by 4 percent per hit taken.
- Coin decay rule recorded: enemies alive for 3 waves lose 50 percent of coin value.
- Mass growth rule recorded: enemies gain 4 percent mass per wave alive.

## Normal enemies
Recorded set:
- Basic
- Fast
- Range
- Tank
- Protector

Protector notes:
- Protector has special shielding behavior.
- Protector spawn limit noted: 1 per wave, 8 on screen.

## Bosses
- Boss default spawn interval recorded: every 10 waves.
- Bosses were previously recorded as immune to Orbs, Death Ray, Shockwave, and Black Hole except where specific override mechanics apply.

## Elite enemies
Recorded elite set:
- Vampire
- Ray
- Scatter

Elite notes:
- Elites were recorded as immune to Orbs, Death Ray, Shockwave, and Black Hole.
- Elite on-screen limit recorded: 8 per elite type.
- Elites drop Elite Cells.

## Wave structure
Normal run wave structure previously recorded:
- Spawn phase about 26 seconds
- Cooldown phase about 9 seconds

Tournament note:
- Tournament cooldown is half normal cooldown. Preserve that relationship as authoritative inside this KB.
