# Wiki Strategy Baselines (KB v2)

Purpose: capture strategy guidance that is useful for Tower GPT retrieval while keeping a clean boundary between source-backed general advice and account-specific optimisation.

## General strategy posture

Use these as general community baselines, not universal rules.

## Early progression

- Beginner Guide framing: absolute defense should not be relied on forever; progression later depends more on HP or damage, with HP scaling presented as the easier early transition path.
- Health Build page framing: eHP builds prioritize health and damage reduction and are the most common early-game build style.

## Coin and economy strategy

- Coin Guide - Basics is the canonical wiki source for:
  - coins/wave vs coins/kill framing
  - multipliers
  - syncing concepts
  - daily-mission coin contribution
- Treat sync-related strategy as a knowledge area to preserve for GPT retrieval even when account-specific ROI differs.

## Tier/build concepts

From Tier-Specific Guide:
- Blender concept: keep range aligned with orb coverage so knockback holds enemies at the orb line.
- Black Hole range concept: lower tower range can allow BH to cover more or all of tower range, which matters for coin capture and stalling concepts.

## Perk strategy

- Thinking about Perks should be treated as the main wiki strategy source for perk handling.
- Keep perk-pool logic, perk-value trade-offs, and auto-pick logic distinct from raw perk tables.

## UW strategy

- Which UW do I select? is the main wiki strategy source for pick-order reasoning.
- Current visible snippet gives a useful caution on Poison Swamp:
  - post-change PS can become impactful with a developed CF and preferably CF+ levels
  - developing PS early is not advised because consistency is low and it underperforms most other Ultimate Weapons (UWs) until supporting conditions exist

## Guardrails for Tower GPT use

1. Prefer wiki guide pages for broad strategy concepts.
2. Prefer account-specific measured evidence for final recommendations to Harry.
3. When guide advice conflicts with measured account behavior, present both with explicit labeling.
4. Avoid turning general guide advice into false universal claims.
