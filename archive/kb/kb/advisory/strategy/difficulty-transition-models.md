# Difficulty Transition Models

## Early game
Primary tools:
- HP
- Defense
- Thorns
- Orbs

## Mid game
Pressure sources:
- Protectors
- Elites
- higher HP scaling
- reduced orb reliability

## Late game
Dominant power layers:
- damage scaling
- Ultimate Weapons
- card masteries
- stronger modules
- better sync / economy support depending on mode
