# Tournament Strategy Framework

## Tournament differences from farming
- faster wave pacing
- harsher battle conditions
- stronger pressure on burst handling
- higher value on control and resilience

## Primary tournament objectives
- survive deeper waves
- retain control under BC pressure
- maintain boss kill reliability
- reduce failure variance

## Battle-condition interpretation rules
### Resistances
Orb, thorns, death ray, and plasma-cannon resistances attack specific archetypes directly.

### Pressure BCs
Enemy speed, attack speed, more enemies, and more bosses raise incoming pressure or compress response windows.

### Rule-change BCs
Enemy ultimates and UW duration reductions alter mechanic behavior rather than just multiplying stats.

## Recommended evaluation order
1. Which BC is invalidating the current build?
2. Is failure caused by control loss or raw damage insufficiency?
3. Which modules / Ultimate Weapons (UWs) / cards resist that BC best?
4. Does the best farming setup remain appropriate, or should the loadout diverge?
