# Wiki source-closure baselines

This note records the module facts now treated as externally source-closed from the wiki:
- Epic modules drawn as epic have unique effects; merged rares do not gain unique effects.
- Assist modules unlock at Tier 19 Wave 40, cost 1000 stones per assist slot unlock, and unique-effect rarity upgrades total 4600 stones across Epic/Legendary/Mythic/Ancestral.
- Assist effects do not bypass listed hard caps such as 98% Defense, 40% Death Defy, 7s Shockwave Frequency, and 37s ILM cooldown.
- The unique-effect values and notes for module uniques are source-closed against the Epic Module Unique Effect wiki table.
