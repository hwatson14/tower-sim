# Tier Difficulty Scaling

Status: structural summary only.

## Canonical rule for numeric work
Do not derive tier difficulty from a generic multiplier model when the raw enemy HP/damage tables are available.

## Better framing
- Farming tiers and tournament leagues are distinct named surfaces in the imported raw tables.
- Base enemy power should come from those tables.
- Additional difficulty then comes from battle conditions, heat, bosses, elites, fleets, and runtime mechanic interactions.
