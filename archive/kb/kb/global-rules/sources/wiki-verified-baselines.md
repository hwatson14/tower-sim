# Wiki-Verified Baselines (KB v2)

This file captures only high-confidence baseline facts checked against the Tower wiki during this KB pass.

## Progression and structure

- The game has 21 tiers.
- The next tier is unlocked by reaching wave 100 on the current tier, except tiers 16-21 which require wave 300 on the previous tier.
- Tournaments unlock at Tier 1 Wave 60.
- Tournaments use 6 leagues: Copper, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Champion, Legend.
- Tournaments occur every Wednesday and Saturday at 0:00 UTC.

## Labs

- Labs unlock at Tier 1 Wave 30.
- Up to 5 labs can be unlocked.
- Total cost to unlock all 5 labs is 4,900 gems.
- Lab slot costs are: Free, 100, 400, 1,400, 3,000 gems.

## Modules

- Modules unlock at Tier 2 Wave 90.
- There are 4 module families:
  - Cannon (Attack)
  - Armor (Defense)
  - Generator (Utility)
  - Core (Ultimate Weapons)

## Enemy categories and caps

- Enemy categories are Normal, Elite, Fleet, and Boss.
- Maximum simultaneous spawn cap is 150 total:
  - 120 Normal
  - 20 Elite
  - 10 Boss
- Fleet enemies occupy the Normal spawn cap.
- Enemies gain 4% more damage per hit via the heat-up mechanic.

## Enemy Level Skip

- Enemy Level Skip unlocks in workshop after Recovery Packages for 1B coins.
- It unlocks Enemy Attack Level Skip and Enemy Health Level Skip.
- Both workshops have 699 levels.
- Base chance is 0.05%.
- Each level adds 0.05%.
- Max chance is 35.00%.

## Perks

### Standard perks (selected baseline examples)
- x1.20 Max Health, quantity 5
- x1.15 Damage, quantity 5
- x1.15 All Coin Bonuses, quantity 5
- x1.75 Health Regen, quantity 5
- Defense Percent +4.00, quantity 5
- Free Upgrade Chance for All +5.0%, quantity 5
- Orbs +1, quantity 2
- Increase Max Game Speed by +1.00, quantity 1
- Unlock a Random Ultimate Weapon, quantity 1

### Perk rules
- Standard perk bonuses can be increased using the Standard Perk Bonus lab.
- At max Standard Perk Bonus lab, the Game Speed perk becomes +1.25 and displayed max game speed becomes 6.3.
- Ultimate weapon perks are shown only when the affected UW is unlocked.
- If a UW is unlocked through the random UW perk during a run, the matching UW perk can then appear for that run.
- The Golden Tower Bonus perk does not apply to the GT bonus submodule effect.

## Ultimate Weapons and Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+)

- Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) can only be unlocked after all 9 Ultimate Weapons are unlocked.
- Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) abilities can be bought in any order.
- Base unlock level is level 0.
- The 9 sequential Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) unlock costs are:
  - 500
  - 625
  - 750
  - 975
  - 1250
  - 1650
  - 2200
  - 2900
  - 3800
- Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) abilities can be upgraded 10 times after unlock.
