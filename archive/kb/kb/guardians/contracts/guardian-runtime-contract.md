# Guardian runtime contract

Guardian is a guild-linked companion system with chip-based timed effects.

Ownership:
- unlock cost and slot/chip baseline
- chip family identity
- chip level / upgrade tables
- timed chip semantics

Rules:
- Guardian unlocks with one chip slot and two base chips: Ally and Attack
- bits are the primary spend resource for Guardian progression
- chips are effect-specific timed mechanics, not ordinary scalar tower stats
- guardian quantitative surfaces should be read from domain tables and not collapsed into generic economy stats
