# Ultimate weapon plus runtime contract

## Baseline semantics
- Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) unlocks only after all 9 Ultimate Weapons are unlocked.
- Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) abilities can be purchased in any order.
- Each additional Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) unlock increases the cost of the next one.
- Newly unlocked Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) abilities start at level 0.

## Domain ownership
Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) remains in the Ultimate Weapons domain because it is a progression extension of UW systems rather than a separate global system.
