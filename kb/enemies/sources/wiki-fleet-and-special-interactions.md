# Wiki Fleet and Special Interactions

Status: rebuilt from prior chat notes, with live wiki spawn notes verified 2026-06-13.

## Fleet enemies
Recorded fleet set:
- Saboteur
- Commander
- Overcharge

## Recorded fleet interaction rules
Live wiki notes currently state that fleet enemies always spawn on their scheduled wave,
including when the wave is skipped. Fleet enemies spawn with a related enemy group, and
the related enemy group count appears random in the 10-14 range. This is source-backed
as a pressure-driver input only; it is not a terminal max-wave formula.

Fleet enemies were previously recorded as immune to:
- Orbs
- Death Ray
- Shockwave
- Knockback
- Black Hole Damage
- Black Hole Pull

Additional recorded resistances/notes:
- 90% Thorns resistance
- 50% resistance to several control effects
- cannot be targeted by Magnetic Hook
- cannot be targeted by Plasma Cannon
- do not collide with other enemies

## Recorded special abilities
- Saboteur disables a random UW on impact until it dies.
- Commander provides a 2x enemy-health buff in an area.
