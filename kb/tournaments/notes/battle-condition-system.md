# Battle Condition System

Battle Conditions are enemy difficulty modifiers that become central in high tiers and tournaments.

## Recorded example conditions
- Death Ray Resistance
- Thorns Resistance
- Enemy Attack Speed
- Knockback Resistance
- Plasma Cannon Resistance
- Orb Resistance
- More Enemies
- Ultimate Weapon Duration

## Strategic implications
- Orb Resistance weakens orb-centric control/funneling.
- Thorns Resistance weakens eHP / thorn reliance.
- Enemy Attack Speed increases survivability pressure.
