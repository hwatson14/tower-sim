# Terminology aliases

## Canonical usage rule
Use the canonical term in active KB surfaces. Short forms are allowed in compact notes, formulas, or table cells only if they are listed here.

## Verified canonical terms and aliases

### Enhancements
- **Canonical term:** Enhancements
- **Aliases:** Workshop Plus, Workshop Enhancement, Workshop Enhancements, WS+
- **Scope note:** This is the permanent post-workshop multiplier layer above base Workshop progression.

### Ultimate Weapons
- **Canonical term:** Ultimate Weapons
- **Aliases:** UW, UWs
- **Scope note:** Refers to the nine Ultimate Weapons as a system.

### Ultimate Weapon Plus

## Policy
- In active filenames and headings, prefer the canonical term.

- In raw source captures, preserve the source wording.
