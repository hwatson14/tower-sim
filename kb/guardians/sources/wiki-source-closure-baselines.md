# Wiki source-closure baselines

This note records guardian facts now treated as externally source-closed from the wiki:
- Guardian is unlocked for 200 bits.
- Unlock gives 1 chip slot and 2 base chips: Ally and Attack.
- Additional chips documented on the wiki are Bounty, Fetch, and Summon.
- Base cooldown/effect semantics for those chips are source-closed from the Guardian and Guardian Guide pages.
