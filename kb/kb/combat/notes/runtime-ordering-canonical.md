# Runtime Ordering Canonical Surface

Use this cluster for combat ordering questions:
- `contracts/runtime-ordering-canonical.csv` for the concise rule surface
- `contracts/runtime-damage-pipeline.csv` for staged combat flow
- `sources/runtime-ordering-evidence.md` for evidence wording

## What is materially closed
- Defense Percent before Defense Absolute
- Separate DR bundle after Defense Absolute
- Wall-first routing while wall is alive
- Contact damage then thorn-trigger resolution on the same contact event

## What remains open
- same-tick tie-break precedence between simultaneous effects
- expiry precedence when a timed effect ends on the same moment another event resolves
- Boss Orb Boss Hit same-moment precedence versus other damage

## Rule
Carry evidence tier explicitly.
Do not promote user-observed timing behavior into exact hidden-engine same-tick canon.
