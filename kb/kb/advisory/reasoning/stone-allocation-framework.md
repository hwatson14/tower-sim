# Stone Allocation Framework

## Purpose
Provide a GPT-readable framework for Ultimate Weapon purchase and upgrade decisions.

## Priority logic
Stone spending should be judged by marginal improvement to one or more of:
- coin generation
- survivability extension
- damage conversion
- tournament competitiveness

## Major stone families
- new UW purchase
- cooldown reduction
- duration increase
- bonus / multiplier increase
- quantity / angle / count expansion
- Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) unlock / improvement

## High-level heuristics
### Economy-first accounts
Generally prefer Golden Tower, Black Hole, Spotlight, and supporting cooldown / duration structures.

### Survival-constrained accounts
Prefer Chrono Field, Black Hole survivability contribution, Death Wave health/cell support, and control-enabling effects.

### Tournament-constrained accounts
Prefer damage conversion, control uptime, boss handling, and battle-condition resilient mechanics.

## Decision tree
1. Identify current bottleneck.
2. Ask whether a new UW or upgrading an existing UW solves it better.
3. Prefer multiplicative synergy over isolated value.
4. Prefer threshold unlocks over tiny linear improvements.
5. Avoid spending into mechanics your build cannot yet leverage.

## Threshold examples
- GT/BH sync value
- Spotlight coverage breakpoints
- Chrono Field uptime breakpoints
- Death Wave tagging thresholds
- Black Hole size / duration windows
