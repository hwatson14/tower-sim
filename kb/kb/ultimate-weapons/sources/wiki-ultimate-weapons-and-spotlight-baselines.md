Status: wiki-backed baseline summary
Confidence: mixed high / medium

## Ultimate Weapons baseline
The main Ultimate Weapons page exposes upgrade ranges for at least some Ultimate Weapons (UWs) directly on-page.

### Black Hole
- Size: 30m to 70m
- Duration: 15s to 38s
- Cooldown: 200s to 50s

### Spotlight
- Bonus: x8.0 to x43.0
- Angle: 30 to 90
- Quantity: 1 to 4

## Ultimate Weapon Plus baseline
Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) abilities:
- require all 9 Ultimate Weapons (UWs) to be unlocked first
- can be purchased in any order
- start at level 0 when unlocked

Unlock cost ladder:
500, 625, 750, 975, 1250, 1650, 2200, 2900, 3800

## Spotlight-specific wiki baselines
### Spotlight Coin Bonus lab
- Applies a coin multiplier to enemies killed in spotlight
- Enemy does not need to be killed by spotlight itself
- Levels 1 to 5 extracted into `wiki_spotlight_coin_bonus_levels_1_5_verified.csv`

### Spotlight Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) page
The Spotlight Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) page states that Light Range boosts Spotlight damage bonus by a percent of damage per meter and that Tower Range affects this ability.

## Strategy note
The Tier-Specific Guide describes Spotlight as a late-game strength that needs heavy investment and notes a T7 W80 coin spike point; treat this as strategy guidance rather than hard law.
