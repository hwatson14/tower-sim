# Wiki bot baselines

## Verified structural facts
- Flame Bot and Thunder Bot are unlocked and upgraded in the Event Shop.
- Unlock cost depends on how many bots have already been bought.
- Flame Bot base upgrade tracks start from medal-funded ladders and increase from a base cost of 100 medals with +40 medals per level.
- Golden Bot duration, cooldown and bonus all have explicit wiki ladder descriptions.
- Bot lab extensions exist, including Golden Bot Duration.
