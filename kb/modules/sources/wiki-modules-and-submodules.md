# Wiki Modules and Submodules Baselines

Status: wiki-backed baseline summary
Confidence: medium-high for listed facts
Use: retrieval and reasoning anchor, not full per-module unique catalog replacement

## Core module system baselines

- Modules unlock at Tier 2 Wave 90.
- There are 4 types of modules: Cannon, Armor, Generator, Core.
- Modules can be acquired with module tickets or gems.
- Gem purchase rates are 20 gems for x1 draw and 200 gems for x10 draws.
- The main Modules page says 10 module tickets are given when modules first unlock.

## Banner and pull rules

- Standard banner uses normal epic rates.
- Featured banner gives a specified module 50% of epic pulls.
- Featured banners last 2 weeks followed by a 1 week break.

## Module rarity rates

- Common: 68.5%
- Rare: 29%
- Epic: 2.5%

## Protection rules

- Guaranteed epic if no epic appears in 150 purchases.
- Guaranteed at least 1 rare in every 10-pull.

## Merging baselines

- Natural Common modules cannot be merged.
- Natural Rare modules can be merged to Legendary+.
- Natural Epic modules can be merged to Ancestral with 5 stars.
- Merging boosts main stat value, main stat scaling, and module max level.
- Rare modules never gain a unique ability from merging, even if merged to Epic or higher.
- Each ancestral star requires a natural Epic+ module and increases the main effect.

## Shards and shattering

- Shattering yields 5 shards for a Common and 10 for a Rare.
- Natural Epics may be shattered after a 5-star Ancestral of the same type is formed.

## Sub-module baselines

- Sub-module rarity is independent of module rarity.
- Sub-modules can be rerolled with reroll shards.
- Existing sub-modules can be locked, which increases reroll cost.
- Assist sub-module effects do not bypass hard caps.

Caps explicitly noted on the Sub-Module Effects page during this pass:
- Defense 98%
- Chrono Field Speed reduction 90%
- Wall rebuild 150s
- Shockwave Frequency 7s
- Death Defy 40%
- Inner Land Mine CD 37s
