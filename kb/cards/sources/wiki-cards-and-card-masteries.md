# Wiki Cards and Card Masteries Baselines

Status: wiki-backed baseline summary
Confidence: medium-high for the facts listed here
Use: retrieval and reasoning anchor, not full per-card exhaustive canon yet

## Core card system baselines

- Cards cost 20 gems per purchase.
- Cards have 3 rarities: Common, Rare, Epic.
- Buy rates on the main Cards page are Common 80%, Rare 17%, Epic 3%.
- Cards have a max level of 7.
- Locked cards cannot be removed during a run.
- When a boss or fleet enemy is alive, all cards become locked until those enemies are cleared.

## Card slot baselines

- Max gem-purchased card slots on the Cards page: 22.
- Total gem cost to reach 22 gem slots: 58,400 gems.
- The page also notes 28 total slots with Harmony tech-tree Key upgrades.

## Maxing cards

- Maxing one card requires 80 copies total.
- Total gem cost per maxed card is 1,600 gems.
- The page reports 31 cards total and 49,600 gems to max all cards.

## Card milestone unlocks on the Cards page

- Recovery Package Chance: Rare, Tier 2 Wave 750
- Land Mine Stun: Rare, Tier 7 Wave 250
- Nuke: Epic, Tier 11 Wave 10
- Ultimate Crit: Epic, Tier 14 Wave 50
- Area of Effect: Epic, Tier 20 Wave 80

## Card Masteries baselines

- Card Masteries unlock when 30 cards are maxed and the Card Mastery Milestone at Tier 16 Wave 100 is unlocked.
- A card must be equipped for its mastery effect to be active.
- Unlocking a Card Mastery costs stones and unlocks that card's Mastery Labs.
- All card mastery labs share the same time/coin table.
